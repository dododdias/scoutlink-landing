FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to speak English to use ScoutLink?▾
ScoutLink is available in both English and Portuguese. Our AI assistant can also help you write emails and messages to coaches in English, even if you're still learning.
How do I get noticed by coaches?▾
Complete your profile with your highlight video, academic stats, and sports achievements. Coaches search by sport, position, GPA, and TOEFL — a complete profile maximizes your chances.
What is NCAA eligibility?▾
NCAA eligibility certifies international student-athletes to compete in US college sports. Our courses and AI assistant guide you through every step of the clearinghouse registration.
Is ScoutLink free?▾
Yes — creating a profile, browsing colleges, messaging coaches, and using core features is completely free. A Pro plan with advanced features is coming soon.
Which sports are supported?▾
ScoutLink supports all major sports including soccer, basketball, volleyball, swimming, track & field, baseball, tennis, and more.
